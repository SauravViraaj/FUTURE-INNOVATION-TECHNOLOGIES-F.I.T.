Frequently asked questions

Things clients often ask.

Every project is different. These answers set the starting point for a useful conversation.

Does F.I.T. supply drones and related equipment?

Use this website to enquire about UAVs, payloads, drone accessories, education kits and related technology. Availability, approved models, sourcing, warranty and delivery lead times should be confirmed in a written quotation.

Can you set up a complete robotics or STEM lab?

We can discuss a lab scope covering requirements, equipment selection, layout, curriculum alignment, installation, educator training and support. The final proposal depends on institution, budget and lab categories.

Do you support custom drone or robotics projects?

Project discovery can establish feasibility, target performance, environment, payload, integration, testing and compliance constraints. Engineering scope and deliverables should be confirmed after technical evaluation.

Do drone projects need permissions in India?

Requirements depend on drone category, intended operation, location and current rules. Operators should verify applicable DGCA requirements, airspace restrictions, registrations and permissions before a flight.

How can I request a quotation?

Send your name, organisation, location, solution, intended application, quantity and target timeline using the enquiry form. Include any specifications or tender document details available.

Do you offer training and after-sales support?

Training and support options can be scoped according to equipment, user group, location and project requirements. Ask for coverage, duration, exclusions and escalation process in the proposal.
